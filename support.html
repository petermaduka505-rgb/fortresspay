Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send money to another account?

To send money, navigate to the Transfer section from the bottom navigation. Enter the recipient's account number and routing number, specify the amount, and confirm your transfer.

Transfers between Fortress Pay accounts are typically instant. External bank transfers settle within 1–3 business days depending on the receiving institution.

Why is my account showing a restricted status?

Accounts may be temporarily restricted due to unusual activity, a pending identity verification, or a compliance review. During a restriction, certain features such as outbound transfers may be unavailable.

Please contact our support team via secure message or phone to resolve the restriction. Have your account number and a valid government-issued ID ready.

How long does it take for a deposit to become available?

Direct deposits are typically available the same day they are received, often before standard banking hours. ACH transfers initiated externally are available within 1–2 business days.

Check deposits made via mobile capture may have a hold period of up to 5 business days for new accounts or large amounts.

How do I update my contact information?

To update your email address or phone number, visit the Profile page. Changes to your primary email require re-verification for security purposes.

For address changes, please contact our support team as identity verification may be required before the update is applied.

What should I do if I notice an unauthorized transaction?

If you see a transaction you do not recognize, contact us immediately via phone or secure message. You can also lock your account from the Profile page under Security while you investigate.

You have 60 days from the date of a statement to dispute unauthorized transactions. We will initiate a provisional credit while we investigate your claim.

How do I set up two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be configured under Profile > Security. We support SMS-based verification. Once enabled, a one-time code will be required each time you sign in from an unrecognized device.

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA to protect your account from unauthorized access.

Can I link an external bank account?

Yes. Navigate to Transfers > Linked Accounts and enter your external bank's routing and account number. We will send two small verification deposits within 1–2 business days which you must confirm to activate the link.

Linked external accounts can be used for funding and withdrawals subject to your daily transfer limits.

What are the daily transfer limits?

Standard accounts have a daily outbound transfer limit of $5,000 and a 30-day rolling limit of $25,000. Limits may differ based on your account tier and verification status.

To request a limit increase, submit a request through secure message. Additional verification may be required.
